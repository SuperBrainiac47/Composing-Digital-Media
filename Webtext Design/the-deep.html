the reef · part three

The reef was never the whole ocean. It was a single room in it.

Out here, scale stops making sense the way it used to. A shape passes far below you, longer than your entire reef, moving without any hurry — a whale, unbothered by anything your size. Something else circles at a distance, all muscle and intention, and you understand fear again, the real kind, not the flinching kind.

But it's not only monsters. The deep is absurdly, unnecessarily full:

The whale: bigger than your entire known world, moving through it like it's nothing
The shark: the fear you didn't have a name for back home
The bioluminescent jelly: light with no sun behind it, glowing on its own
The giant squid: something the reef has no word for at all

Nobody in the school ever mentioned any of this. Not because they were hiding it. They genuinely didn't know. You didn't either, an hour ago.

You could stay out here. Nothing's forcing you back — the deep doesn't care whether you leave. Most fish who make it this far never do go back.

Swim back and tell them Stay in the deep
