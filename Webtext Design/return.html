the reef · part four

You're back in the maze. Nothing here has changed size.

You try to explain it, the drop-off, the whale, the terrors, and the vastness. You reach for words the reef never needed before, never created before. Try telling one of them.

Tell the school of guppies
Tell whoever's in the coral maze
Tell the cleaner shrimp

This isn't broken. It's the point, the reef and its inhabitants have no ability to fathom what you discovered.
