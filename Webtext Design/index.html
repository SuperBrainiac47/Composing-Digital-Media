the reef · part one

This is the whole ocean, as far as you know.

You've lived your whole life in this one section of the reef. The light reaches all the way to the sand here, the water is warm, and you know every rock well enough to hide in it without looking.

You've never had a reason to think there's more than this. Everyone you know lives here too.

The school of guppies the other small fish you grew up around The coral maze the walls that have always marked the edges of "home" The cleaning station the shrimp and the routine you never questioned

Go look around. It's all familiar. That's the point.
