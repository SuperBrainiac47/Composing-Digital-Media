the reef · part two

The coral just... stops here

You found a gap. One you've never seen before. One no one has ever seen befpre. Past the last branch of coral, the sand shelf drops away into blue that keeps going further into the expanse.

It's not just dark. It's just enormous, in a way the reef never prepared you to expect. Something in you wants to turn back to the school, the maze, the shrimp. Something else wants to know what's actually out there.
