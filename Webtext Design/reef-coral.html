The walls have always just been the edge of the world."

The coral branches into a maze you know by feel, which gap to dart through, which ledge has an eel under it, which turn leads back to the sand you sleep in. You built your whole world in this maze.

You've always assumed there is nothing past the edge of the reef. Not just you everyone believed the same.

A map of your entire world was in that reef and you've never needed it to be bigger because thats all you knew.
