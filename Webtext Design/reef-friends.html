the known world: the school

Everyone you've ever met is this size.

The fish move together, turn together, eat together. Nobody in the school has ever been far from where they hatched. Nobody's thought not to be.

When something big passes far overhead, a giant shadow, the whole school flinches the same way, at the same time, without anyone actually knowing what it was. That's just what you do out here.

It's comfortable. It's also the only reference point you have for "normal."
