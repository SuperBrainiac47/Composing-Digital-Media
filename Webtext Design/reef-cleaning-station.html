the known world: the routine

Same shrimp, same rock, same time every day.

You line up at the cleaning station like everyone else. The shrimp pick off the dirt that's bothering you, you hold still, then you swim on. It's a good system. Nobody questions it because it works.

It's a daily ritual, a sense of order, proof that the reef is sustainable on its own. You've built a whole worldview on a routine that never has to change, but that's because nothing makes it change.
