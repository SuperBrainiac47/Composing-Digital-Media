the reef · a different ending

You don't go back.

The reef never finds out what you found. No one back there ever learns there's a whale bigger than their whole world, or a shark, or a light with no sun behind it. They keep flinching at shadows overhead and never knowing why. That was always going to keep happening whether you told them or not.

Out here the water is colder and the scale of things stops being comforting. But it's honest, and can't unsee it now.

This is where the story ends. There's nothing further to click.
